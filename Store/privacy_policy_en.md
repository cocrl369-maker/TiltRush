## 2. General
"Tilt Rush" can be played without an account. Progress, settings and statistics are stored **only on your device**.

## 3. Online features (Unity Gaming Services)
Leaderboards and online play use services of **Unity Technologies SF**, 30 3rd Street, San Francisco, CA 94103, USA ("Unity"): Authentication, Leaderboards, Lobby.
- **Data:** a random anonymous player ID, your chosen player name, your scores / ELO rating, your round times during online matches, technically required connection data (e.g. IP address).
- **Purpose:** leaderboards and online matches. **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Your player name is visible to other players. Do not use your real name. Your player ID is shown in *Options > Profile*.
- Unity privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

## 4. Advertising (Unity Ads)
- **Data:** advertising ID, IP address, device and OS information, language, approximate location (from IP), ad interactions.
- **Purpose:** showing, measuring and billing ads; personalised ads only with your consent.
- **Legal basis:** consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised ads – given or refused at first start, withdraw any time in *Options > Profile > Personalised Ads*; legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalised ads.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings.

## 5. Purchases (Google Play)
The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by **Google Play** (Google Ireland Limited). We receive no payment data. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Transfers outside the EU
Unity also processes data in the USA. Unity Technologies is certified under the **EU-US Data Privacy Framework**; EU standard contractual clauses are used as well.

## 7. Retention
Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or use *Options > Profile > Reset Progress*. Data at Unity is kept as long as needed for leaderboards / online features or until you request deletion.
